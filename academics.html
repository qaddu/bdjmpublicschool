Our Pedagogy

Learning by Doing

At BDJM, we strictly follow the CBSE curriculum, but we deliver it differently. We believe the early and middle years (Classes I to VIII) are the most critical time to build a genuine love for learning.

We have moved away from traditional rote memorization. Instead, our classrooms are interactive spaces where concepts are taught through experiments, visual aids, and project-based learning. We ensure that our students understand the "why" behind the "what."

Our Academic Wings

A structured journey through the foundational years.

Primary Wing (Classes I - V)

Our Primary curriculum focuses on building strong foundations in literacy, numeracy, and environmental awareness. We use play-way methods, storytelling, and interactive activities to ensure learning is joyful and engaging for our youngest minds.

Middle Wing (Classes VI - VIII)

As students enter their pre-teen years, the focus shifts to analytical thinking and conceptual clarity. We introduce advanced Science, Mathematics, and Language concepts through laboratory experiments, group discussions, and comprehensive research projects.
